Thirteen themes. Every layout designed.

Each theme restyles all 30 layouts and passes the same design checks on the same 33-slide lecture. Open one to play it, or see every slide.

Folio

Editorial serif on warm paper — a magazine feature you can present.

Best for essays and keynotes, humanities lectures, strategy narratives.

Nocturne

Dark cinematic keynote — serif headlines glowing amber in a quiet room.

Best for conference keynotes, product vision talks, storytelling.

Grid

Swiss International Style — bold grotesk, strict grid, one cobalt signal.

Best for business reviews, design and architecture talks, startup pitches.

Campus

Academic and clear — a reading-first theme for lectures, seminars and theses.

Best for university lectures, thesis defenses, research seminars.

Atlas

Consulting-grade clarity — action titles, numbered exhibits, navy and gold.

Best for case competitions, strategy and business reviews, board updates.

Aurora

Product-launch glow — wide display type, glass panels and northern-light gradients.

Best for product launches, startup demo days, AI and tech announcements.

Volt

Brutalist terminal energy — monospace grid, black field, acid-lime signal.

Best for developer talks, hackathon demos, tech meetups.

Ma

Japanese minimalism — stillness, generous space and a single indigo note.

Best for design and architecture talks, philosophy and humanities, mindful or wellbeing topics.

Bloom

Warm and playful — rounded shapes, friendly serif and tomato-red energy.

Best for workshops, school and club presentations, community events.

Chalk

A real classroom blackboard — handwritten headings, chalk-yellow highlights.

Best for school lessons, tutoring and revision, maths and science explainers.

Blueprint

An engineering drawing — cyan linework on blueprint blue, a drafting grid and orange redlines.

Best for engineering and architecture talks, system design reviews, hardware and robotics.

Broadsheet

A newspaper front page — heavy serif headlines, double rules, column lines and typewriter datelines.

Best for research findings and reports, policy briefings, journalism and media classes.

Clinic

Clean and reassuring — bright white, calm cyan-teal, rounded panels and highly legible type.

Best for healthcare and medicine, public health briefings, life-science research.
